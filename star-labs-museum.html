Origin File

Every speedster's story starts with a bolt of lightning and a lab full of unstable chemicals. Here's the timeline S.T.A.R. Labs keeps on record.

Childhood

A mystery in Central City

A young forensics enthusiast loses his mother under circumstances nobody believes — a blur of light and color in the living room. His father is convicted for a crime he insists he didn't commit. The unsolved case becomes the reason he grows up chasing the impossible instead of running from it.

The Accident

Struck by lightning, bathed in chemicals

Working late at the Central City Police forensics lab on the night S.T.A.R. Labs' particle accelerator misfires, he's knocked out by a bolt of lightning that douses him in a shelf of chemicals at the same instant. He wakes up nine months later with his cells fundamentally rewritten.

Awakening

A connection to the Speed Force

The accident doesn't just make him fast — it tethers him to an extradimensional energy field that grants speedsters their power. Every time he runs, he draws on it; every time he pushes past his limits, he learns a little more about what it actually is.

Team-Up

S.T.A.R. Labs becomes his home base

The very team responsible for the particle accelerator becomes his support crew — building his suit, monitoring his vitals, and eventually building an AI, Gideon, to help track the impossible physics of his abilities.

Mantle

Becoming the Flash

He starts small: outrunning muggers, catching falling debris before it lands. Word spreads. The city needs a name for the streak of red and yellow lightning saving them at 3 a.m. — and "the Flash" sticks.

02

The Speed Force

S.T.A.R. Labs classifies the Speed Force as an extradimensional energy field rather than a power source in the conventional sense — it doesn't run out so much as it has to be earned back. Speedsters don't generate velocity; they borrow it.

Access to the Force lets a runner do more than sprint: vibrating molecules to phase through solid matter, pulling latent heat from the air to throw ice, or running fast enough to create afterimages and even split into duplicates for a moment. In rare, dangerous cases, speed has been used to cross into other timelines entirely.

The tradeoff is real. Draw too hard on the Force and a speedster risks getting pulled into it permanently — becoming a kind of living conduit rather than a person who can still choose to stop.

Rogues Gallery

A speedster attracts a very particular kind of enemy. S.T.A.R. Labs maintains threat files on the recurring cast of metahumans and criminals built around Central City's fastest resident.

Reverse Speedster

Threat: Extreme

A rival runner from generations ahead, obsessed with the Flash and driven by a grudge that predates their first meeting. Capable of matching the Flash stride for stride and phasing through matter at will.

Classification · Speedster

Cold Gun Engineer

Threat: High

A meticulous career criminal who traded a standard pistol for a thermodynamic freeze gun of his own design. Leads a loose crew of fellow "Rogues" with a strict, old-fashioned code: no killing, no working with speedsters who break their word.

Classification · Tech-Enhanced

Mirror Specialist

Threat: Moderate

Uses reflective glass and light-bending tech to project illusions and false doubles, making a straightforward footrace almost useless — you can't outrun what isn't really there.

Classification · Illusionist

The Turned Friend

Threat: High

A former ally caught in the same particle accelerator blast, left able to transform into a living weapon of shifting metal. The tragedy of the file is that the person underneath never asked for any of it.

Classification · Metahuman

The Weather Broker

Threat: High

Commands storms, lightning, and localized weather systems at will — one of the few adversaries who can threaten an entire city block without ever getting close enough for a footrace.

Gorilla Grade-A

Threat: Extreme

A hyper-intelligent, telepathic gorilla and escapee from a hidden research facility, ruling a secret city of his own kind and treating Central City's heroes as an ongoing chess match.
